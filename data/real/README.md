# Real-camera data availability

The v1.0.0 study used 22 original Samsung SM-R210 side-by-side dual-fisheye JPEGs (5792 × 2896 pixels): one development/calibration image and 21 independent tests. The images are not redistributed in this public repository because some scenes contain identifiable people and redistribution must respect privacy/consent constraints.

Non-image metadata and the study partition are provided in `../../research/REAL_CAMERA_DATASET_METADATA.csv`. The associated manuscript states that original images are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request, subject to privacy and redistribution considerations.
